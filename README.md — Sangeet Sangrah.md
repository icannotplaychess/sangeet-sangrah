# संगीत संग्रह — Sangeet Sangrah

> **स्वर. शब्द. स्मृती.**  
> A digital archive for Marathi music, poetry, artists, and musical culture.

**Sangeet Sangrah** is a Marathi-first music knowledge platform inspired by Wikipedia, digital archives, and editorial music publications.

It brings together information about **Marathi singers, composers, lyricists, poets, songs, films, poetry, musical traditions, history, and cultural context** in one searchable space.

The goal is simple: make Marathi musical knowledge easier to discover, understand, and preserve.

---

## What is Sangeet Sangrah?

Marathi music has an enormous history spanning classical music, natya sangeet, bhavgeet, devotional music, folk traditions, Marathi cinema, poetry, and contemporary music.

A lot of this knowledge is scattered across different places.

**Sangeet Sangrah** aims to create one connected digital collection where users can explore that history.

Think:

**Wikipedia × Music Archive × Marathi Literature × Interactive Discovery**

## Features

### कलाकार — Artists

Explore profiles of:

- गायक / गायिका
- संगीतकार
- गीतकार
- कवी
- कलाकार
- संगीताशी संबंधित इतर व्यक्ती

Each profile can include biographies, careers, notable works, collaborations, and related people.

### गीते — Songs

Discover Marathi songs with information such as:

- गायक / गायिका
- गीतकार
- संगीतकार
- चित्रपट / अल्बम
- वर्ष
- राग
- ताल
- शैली
- ऐतिहासिक संदर्भ

### भावार्थ — Song Meanings

Understand the ideas and cultural context behind Marathi songs.

Articles can explore:

- गीताचा संदर्भ
- भावार्थ
- प्रतिमा आणि रूपके
- सांस्कृतिक संदर्भ
- संगीतशैली

> Sangeet Sangrah focuses on interpretation and knowledge rather than reproducing complete copyrighted lyrics.

### कविता — Poetry

A dedicated space for Marathi poetry, including different poetic traditions and themes.

### शायरी — Shayari

Discover short Marathi poetic expressions organized around themes such as:

- प्रेम
- विरह
- पाऊस
- आठवणी
- निसर्ग
- जीवन

### आजचा तथ्य — Random Trivia

Get a randomly selected fact about Marathi music and its history.

### संगीत क्विझ — Music Quiz

Test your knowledge with quizzes covering:

- Artists
- Songs
- Composers
- Lyricists
- Marathi cinema
- Music history
- Poetry
- Classical music

### काहीही उघडा — Random Discovery

Don't know what to explore?

Let the archive choose.

The random discovery system can take you to an artist, song, poem, composer, historical event, fact, or musical tradition.

### शोध — Search

Search across the entire collection for:

`कलाकार • गीते • कविता • कवी • गीतकार • संगीतकार • चित्रपट • राग • वाद्ये • इतिहास`

The platform is designed around **Devanagari-first search and navigation**.

## Design

Sangeet Sangrah uses a dark, editorial visual identity inspired by contemporary digital magazines and archival websites.

### Visual principles

- Deep midnight background
- Elegant Devanagari serif typography
- High-contrast editorial headlines
- Muted blue-grey text
- Subtle cyan accents
- Thin borders
- Large negative space
- Minimal interface
- Typography-driven layouts
- Restrained animations

The intention is to make the archive feel less like a database and more like a **digital cultural publication**.

---

## Content Structure

The project is designed around interconnected cultural information.

```text
Artist
   ↓
Songs
   ↓
Composer ─── Lyricist
   ↓
Film / Album
   ↓
Year / Era
   ↓
Historical Context
```

Poetry and music can also intersect:

```text
Poet
   ↓
Poems
   ↓
Songs
   ↓
Composer
   ↓
Artist
```

This makes Sangeet Sangrah more than a collection of isolated articles.

It is intended to become a **network of Marathi musical and literary knowledge**.

## Project Goals

Sangeet Sangrah aims to:

1. Preserve knowledge about Marathi music.
2. Make Marathi musical history easier to explore.
3. Connect music with poetry, literature, cinema, and culture.
4. Encourage curiosity through quizzes and random discovery.
5. Create a Marathi-first digital experience.
6. Make cultural research approachable for younger audiences.
7. Build a growing, interconnected archive rather than a static website.
